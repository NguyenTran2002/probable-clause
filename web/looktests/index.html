Probable Clause look tests

Three art directions for the page. Each renders the same sample run (fictional cases, placeholder passages, placeholder notice wording) plus a page of states, so they differ in style only.

  1. Slip opinion

    The page set like the Court's printed opinions: white paper, a Century-like serif, small-caps justices, footnote markers in the answer, and passages in the margin beside the search that found them. On a phone, passages sit inline under that search.

    The runThe states

  2. Cover stock

    Colours from the brief covers Supreme Court Rule 33 assigns, each marking one kind of text: opinion, syllabus, web check, Claude's synthesis. Public Sans with a serif for quoted text. On a phone, a sticky switch between Research and Passages.

    The runThe states

  3. Evidence log

    The research as a chain of custody: a timed log, each passage an exhibit on a manila tag stamped kept or discarded. Condensed Archivo. On a phone, the exhibits ride in a bottom sheet with a running count.

    The runThe states
